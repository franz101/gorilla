    > INSTRUCTIONS
    
      
    Your mission is to hit your opponent with an exploding banana by varying the angle and power of your throw, taking into account wind speed, gravity, and the city skyline.
    
      
    Zero degrees is horizontal toward your opponent, 90° is straight up, 180° is horizontal away. Angle ranges 0–180, velocity 1–200.
    
      
    Only a direct hit on the gorilla scores — explosions on buildings are just for show, exactly like the original. Hit your own gorilla and the point goes to your opponent.
    
      
    Watch the wind arrow at the top: its direction is the drift, its length is the strength.
